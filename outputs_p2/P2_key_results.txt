Honest conclusions:
  1. The raw fidelity curves are non-monotonic; the isotonic envelope is the defensible monotone representation. The violations are reported above.
  2. At τ=0.50: tv 182.6 m (eligible [181.1, 183.9]) vs mod 145.5 m (eligible [141.7, 151.6]); tv is above mod. At τ=0.70: tv 102.2 m (eligible [86.9, 109.4]) vs mod 88.4 m (eligible [82.9, 93.0]); intervals overlap. Flat is clearly lowest at both thresholds; do not rank tv/mod at τ=0.70.
  3. The fitted isotonic curves cross at τ* (reported above). At τ=0.70 the tv-mod difference CI includes 0 unless stated otherwise, so treat tv and mod as indistinguishable at the reliable threshold.
  4. Informative missingness can bias complete-case ranges upward; only bounds with Reliable=TRUE should be quoted as credible. For a call type with no reliable worst/best fit, the range is NOT IDENTIFIED under MNAR and the complete-case value is optimistic (tv is the clear example).
  5. The mean-curve bootstrap CI is not an individual recognition interval; the common-trial bracket (with the monotone logistic as a smooth interpolation) is the appropriate probabilistic statement.
  6. The envelope channel cannot reach 0.70 at 4 m; the composite 0.70 range is a compensatory average, not a channel-wise bottleneck.
  7. p=0.90 is an individual-level guarantee. A short or zero p=0.90 range means fewer than 90% of trials reach tau even at 4 m; report p=0.50/0.75 as the main ecological range and p=0.90 as a strict bound.
